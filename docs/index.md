# MA502: Number Theory

Department of Mathematics, Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, Ranchi. Session MO-2026.

## Notes

- [Module 1: Divisibility & Arithmetic Functions](notes/module-1.md): well-ordering, induction, the division algorithm, GCD, the Euclidean algorithm, linear Diophantine equations, the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, and Euler's \(\varphi\), Möbius \(\mu\), \(\tau\) and \(\sigma\).
- [Module 2: Congruences & Primitive Roots](notes/module-2.md): congruences, residue classes, linear congruences, the Chinese Remainder Theorem, Euler–Fermat, Lagrange's theorem, Wilson's theorem and primitive roots.

## Assignments

- [Assignment 1](assignments/assignment-1.md): Module I, 28 problems.
- [Assignment 2](assignments/assignment-2.md): Module II, 35 problems.
